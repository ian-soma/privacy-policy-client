Soma Customer Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 29, 2025

Hey there! Let's talk about your privacy

We're Soma Health Solutions Inc., and we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our platform. We've tried to keep it straightforward and honest.

What information do we collect?

Information you give us directly

  • Your account details (name, email, professional credentials)
  • Client information you add (aliases you create, notes you write)
  • Transcripts from sessions (audio is processed in real-time only, never stored)
  • Journal entries, mood ratings, and activity data your clients share with you
  • Wearable device data summaries (if your clients consent to share them)

Information we collect automatically

  • How you use the platform (what features you click, when you log in)
  • Technical stuff (your device type, browser, IP address)
  • Error logs and performance data to help us fix bugs

How we keep things anonymous

Here's something important: all client data shared through Soma is anonymous to us. We use the aliases you create for your clients, not their real names. We literally don't know who your clients are unless you tell us in the alias itself (so please don't do that!).

Encryption matters

  • Client journal entries, activity details, and wearable credentials are encrypted
  • We won't decrypt sensitive data without explicit consent
  • Wearable device credentials stay encrypted no matter what—we never decrypt them, even if someone asks us to

What about audio recordings?

Important Update: We process audio in real-time only. When you record a session:

  • You must get explicit consent from your client first
  • Audio is never stored—it's processed live to create the transcript, then immediately discarded
  • Only the resulting transcript is saved to your account
  • We won't use the transcription process for anything else without additional consent

The transcripts? Those are yours to keep and use professionally. But please review them before adding anything to official clinical records—AI isn't perfect.

How we use your information

We use your data to:

  • Provide the services you signed up for (duh!)
  • Generate transcripts in real-time during sessions
  • Create AI-assisted note drafts
  • Send you important updates about the platform
  • Improve our product (with anonymized data)
  • Keep everything secure and running smoothly
  • Comply with legal requirements

We don't sell your data

Let's be clear: we don't sell your information to anyone. Not now, not ever. We're not in the data-selling business.

Who we share data with

We only share data when necessary:

  • Subcontractors: We use trusted third-party services (like cloud hosting and AI transcription APIs) to run the platform. They're all under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal requirements: If we're legally required to disclose information, we will (but we'll tell you first if we can).
  • Your clients: Obviously, if your client shares data with you through the platform, you see it. Once you receive it, you're responsible for how you store and use it according to your professional obligations.

Your rights and controls

You have control over your data:

  • Access: You can see what data we have about you
  • Correction: Fix any incorrect information
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your data (though we may need to keep some for legal reasons)
  • Export: Get a copy of your data to take elsewhere
  • Stop sharing: You or your clients can disconnect at any time

How we protect your information

Security is a big deal to us:

  • Industry-standard encryption for data in transit and at rest
  • Regular security audits and monitoring
  • Limited access—only authorized personnel can see sensitive data
  • Secure cloud infrastructure with redundant backups
  • We follow healthcare data protection standards
  • Real-time audio processing means no audio storage vulnerability

Data breaches—what if something goes wrong?

If there's ever a data breach involving your information:

  • We'll tell you immediately (definitely within the timeframe required by law)
  • We'll explain what happened and what data was affected
  • We'll let you know what we're doing to fix it
  • We'll help you with any notifications you need to make

Wearable device data

If your clients connect wearable devices:

  • It's completely optional—they can say no
  • Data stays anonymous and encrypted
  • We only share summaries with you if clients explicitly consent
  • Clients can use this data to improve our product if they want (also optional)

Changes to this policy

We might update this policy occasionally. If we make big changes that reduce your privacy protections, we'll email you 30 days in advance. You'll have time to review and decide if you want to continue using Soma.

For minor updates, we'll post them here and note the date at the top.

Children's privacy

Our platform isn't designed for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you're a healthcare provider working with minors, you're responsible for obtaining proper parental consent.

Data retention

  • Active accounts: We keep your data as long as your account is active
  • After termination: You have 60 days to request your data. After that, we'll permanently delete everything unless we're legally required to keep it
  • Audio recordings: Never stored—processed in real-time only
  • Transcripts: Kept until you delete them or close your account

International users

We're based in Canada, and our servers are located in North America. If you're using Soma from another country, your data will be processed here. We follow Canadian privacy laws, which are pretty strict.

Your responsibilities

Remember, you're a healthcare professional:

  • You're responsible for getting proper consent before recording
  • You must review AI-generated content before using it clinically
  • You need to follow your professional obligations and local laws
  • If you choose aliases that could identify clients, that's on you

2.4 Shared Terms – Client Connection, Data Sharing, Audio Recording, and Wearable Device Data

(a) Optional Connection – A connection between a client's Soma mobile app account and a Provider's Soma professional account is strictly optional. Either the client or the Provider may end the connection at any time through the Platform settings or by contacting Soma.

(b) Client Control of Shared Data – The client determines which information is shared with the Provider through Soma. This may include:

  • Journal entries
  • Mood or stress ratings
  • Time, date, duration, and completion states of activities
  • Audio transcripts (if separately consented)
  • Wearable device data (if separately consented)

Soma will only transmit the specific categories of data that the client has explicitly chosen to share.

(c) Anonymity and Encryption –

(i) All client data shared through Soma is anonymous to Soma, and where technically feasible, is pseudonymized using Provider-assigned aliases.

(ii) Sensitive client data, including journal entries, activity details, and wearable device credentials, is encrypted. Soma will not decrypt such data without the client's explicit consent, and in the case of wearable credentials, Soma will not decrypt them under any circumstances, even if consent is provided for product improvement.

(d) Audio Recording Rules – If a Provider records a client session for transcription purposes:

(i) The Provider must obtain explicit consent from the client before recording begins. Such consent may be obtained in writing, electronically, or through Soma's consent mechanism.

(ii) Audio is processed in real-time only and is not stored. Audio streams are used solely to create transcripts in real-time, and the audio data is immediately discarded after processing.

(iii) Only the resulting transcripts are retained and may be summarized for clinical note drafts.

(iv) Transcripts will not be used for any other purpose without the client's further explicit consent.

(e) Wearable Device Data Rules – If a Provider requests wearable device data:

(i) The client's connection to a wearable device is optional and may be refused even if the Provider has enabled wearable integration as an option.

(ii) Any wearable data collected through Soma remains anonymous.

(iii) The types of wearable data that may be collected include, without limitation, heart rate, oxygen levels, breathing rate, sleep data, temperature, and activity data.

(iv) Soma will summarize and share wearable data with the Provider only if the client has explicitly consented to connect a wearable device.

(v) If the client consents to anonymous wearable data collection, Soma may use such data to improve the product in addition to any other data the client has consented to share for improvement purposes.

(f) Provider Responsibility for Shared Data – Once any shared data (including journal entries, mood ratings, activity completion states, transcripts, or wearable data summaries) is received by a Provider, the Provider is solely responsible for:

(i) Storing, securing, and using the data in compliance with all applicable laws, regulations, and professional standards; and

(ii) Retaining or disposing of such data according to applicable retention requirements. Soma's role is limited to secure transmission, anonymization, encryption, and real-time audio processing where applicable, as described in this Agreement.

(g) Disputes – Any disputes or concerns regarding the use of shared data after receipt by a Provider are between the Provider and the client. Soma may confirm the type and timing of shared data but is not responsible for how the Provider stores, secures, or uses such data thereafter.

4.10 Reports v2 Module – Data Lifecycle.

Customer acknowledges that automated de-identification is applied as a safeguard but may not identify all personal identifiers in every instance. Real-time audio streams used for transcription are not de-identified prior to transmission; their primary protections are the real-time-only processing model and the audio non-retention commitment described in Section 2.4(d)(ii), together with the no-retention commitment described in Section 11.3, which is the primary protection for all Customer Data transmitted to Third-Party AI Services.

Customer Data transmitted to Third-Party AI Services via the Platform is processed in real-time solely for the purpose of generating the requested Output. Data is not stored or retained by any Third-Party AI Service after the request is processed and the Output is returned. Soma maintains agreements with all Third-Party AI Services prohibiting them from storing or retaining any Customer Data (including inputs, document data, or any other transmitted information) beyond the time necessary to generate and return Output to Soma.

Customer Data transmitted to Third-Party AI Services will not be used to train, improve, or develop the AI models of those Third-Party AI Services.

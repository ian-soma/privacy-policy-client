SOMA Health — Sub-processors

Last updated: June 8, 2026

This page lists the third-party service providers ("sub-processors") that Soma Health Solutions Inc. ("SOMA") engages to deliver the SOMA Services. Each sub-processor that processes Customer information does so under a written agreement that imposes data-protection, confidentiality, and security obligations at least as strict as SOMA's commitments to its customers, including an executed Business Associate Agreement where Protected Health Information is involved.

The legend below applies to the Data Categories column.

Notice of Changes

SOMA will give customers at least thirty (30) days' prior notice of any addition or replacement of a sub-processor that processes Regulated Data. Customers may object to a proposed change on reasonable data-protection grounds within fifteen (15) days by writing to privacy@soma-health.ca. The procedure and remedies for unresolved objections are set out in the Data Processing Agreement, Section 5.2.

SOMA HEALTH SOLUTIONS INC. – ETHICS AND VALUES POLICY

At Soma Health Solutions Inc., we are committed to building exceptional software for therapists and counselors. We are a for-purpose technology company devoted to the professionals who dedicate their careers to clinical practice. Our values reflect our deep commitment to building tools that serve practitioners with dignity, safety, and clarity.

OUR COMMITMENT TO CLINICAL PROFESSIONALS

We are building tools that eliminate non-value-adding tasks in the professional workflow—so that therapists and counselors can spend more time doing the work they trained for. Our software is designed to:

  • Reduce cognitive load by automating administrative functions;
  • Draft clinical notes, transcribe sessions, and surface relevant patterns across a caseload;
  • Streamline communication workflows so practitioners can stay organized between appointments.

We do this while always keeping the clinician in control and ensuring all outputs are subject to professional review. Data use is de-identified, with opt-in only settings for product improvement.

OUR COMMITMENT TO DATA PRIVACY AND END-USER PROTECTION

We believe every individual whose data passes through our software deserves a digital environment that is secure, respectful of their privacy, and transparent about how information is handled. Soma is designed not to diagnose or make clinical determinations, but to provide a structured, private space for engagement with professional services. Through our mobile application, end users can:

  • Enter data that is completely anonymous;
  • Provide explicit consent on optional features that default to off;
  • Grant or deny individual data-sharing requests from their provider;
  • Use structured journaling features built into the application.

We are committed to protecting user anonymity and upholding full transparency in how data is used, with all personal data fully anonymized. Data use is minimal, with opt-in only settings for anonymous personal content.

OUR ETHICAL FOUNDATION

Soma Health Solutions Inc. is guided by the following core ethical values:

  • Respect and Professionalism: We build tools for people, not cases. Every interaction with Soma technology should respect the dignity of users.
  • Trust and Transparency: We are clear and honest about how our software works, how data is used, and where it is stored.
  • Human Oversight: AI should augment—not replace—professional judgment. Practitioners remain fully responsible for all clinical decisions.
  • Accessibility and Equity: We strive to reduce barriers to efficient clinical workflows by making our tools usable, affordable, and inclusive.
  • Continuous Improvement: We commit to improving our technology based on feedback from practitioners, end users, and the broader professional community.

OUR MISSION

Soma Health Solutions Inc.’s mission is to empower therapists, counselors, and clinical organizations with best-in-class practice management tools. We are dedicated to building innovative software that makes clinical documentation more efficient, measurable, and personalized—powered by artificial intelligence, but grounded in the needs of working professionals.

SOMA Health — Business Associate Agreement

Effective: August 23, 2026

This Business Associate Agreement ("BAA") forms part of the Master Service Agreement, Terms of Service, or other governing services agreement (the "Master Agreement") entered into between Soma Health Solutions Inc. ("Business Associate" or "SOMA") and the customer entity that has executed the Master Agreement ("Covered Entity"). The BAA addresses the parties' respective responsibilities with respect to Protected Health Information ("PHI") under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996, as amended, and its implementing regulations at 45 CFR Parts 160 and 164 (together, the "HIPAA Rules"). Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings given in the HIPAA Rules. This BAA applies only to the extent that Customer Data provided to SOMA under the Master Agreement constitutes Protected Health Information as defined under the HIPAA Rules; it does not apply to Customer Data that does not constitute PHI, which is governed solely by the Master Agreement and any executed Data Processing Agreement.

Section 1 — Uses and Disclosures of PHI

1.1 Services

SOMA provides the Services described in the Master Agreement. In the course of providing those Services, SOMA may receive, create, maintain, or transmit PHI on behalf of Covered Entity.

1.2 General Prohibition and Limitations

SOMA will not use or disclose PHI other than as permitted or required by this BAA, the Master Agreement, or applicable law. SOMA may use or disclose PHI only to the extent the use or disclosure would not violate the HIPAA Rules if performed by Covered Entity, and only to the minimum extent necessary to perform SOMA’s obligations under the Master Agreement. Where SOMA is subject to professional or legal obligations that conflict with a restriction in this BAA, those obligations control.

1.3 Business Associate's Use of PHI

SOMA may use PHI internally as reasonably necessary for the proper management and administration of SOMA's business and to carry out its legal responsibilities.

1.4 Business Associate's Disclosure of PHI

SOMA may disclose PHI for the management and administration of its business or to carry out its legal responsibilities only where the disclosure is required by law, or where the recipient provides written assurances that the recipient will: (i) hold the PHI in confidence and use or further disclose it only as required by law or for the purpose for which it was disclosed; (ii) notify SOMA of any instance of which the recipient becomes aware in which the confidentiality of the PHI has been breached.

1.5 Safeguards

1.5.1 Privacy Safeguards. SOMA will develop, implement, and maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to prevent the use or disclosure of PHI other than as permitted by this BAA, consistent with the requirements of the HIPAA Privacy Rule.

1.5.2 Security Rule Compliance. SOMA will comply with the applicable provisions of the HIPAA Security Rule (45 CFR Part 164, Subpart C) and will implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards reasonably designed to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of electronic PHI that it creates, receives, maintains, or transmits on behalf of Covered Entity.

1.6 Subcontractors

SOMA will ensure that any subcontractor that creates, receives, maintains, or transmits PHI on its behalf agrees in writing to restrictions and conditions at least as protective as those imposed on SOMA under this BAA, including the obligation to enter into a written agreement that satisfies the HIPAA Rules.

1.7 Prohibition on Sale of PHI

SOMA will not sell PHI as that term is defined under the HIPAA Rules.

1.8 De-Identified PHI

SOMA may de-identify PHI in accordance with 45 CFR § 164.514(b). Once de-identified, the resulting information is no longer PHI and may be used for any lawful purpose, subject to the further limitations in Section 1.9.

1.9 Use of Third-Party Artificial-Intelligence Sub-processors

SOMA discloses that it uses (i) Anthropic, PBC large-language models, accessed through Amazon Web Services Bedrock (storage and compute in Canada, ca-central-1; transient inference may execute in U.S. AWS Regions), and (ii) Google LLC large-language models, accessed through Google Cloud Vertex in Canada (northamerica-northeast1, Montreal), to provide certain artificial-intelligence features within the Services. SOMA has executed a Business Associate Agreement with Amazon Web Services and a Data Processing Addendum with Google LLC, and has contractually committed to the obligation that Anthropic, as a Bedrock model provider, and Google, as the Vertex model provider, do not retain customer prompts or completions and do not use customer Regulated Data to train, fine-tune, or improve any model.

Notwithstanding Section 1.8, SOMA will not use customer PHI or de-identified data derived from customer PHI to train, fine-tune, evaluate, or improve any artificial-intelligence model, whether SOMA's own model or that of any sub-processor, without Covered Entity's prior written authorization. Covered Entity may request copies of SOMA's executed sub-processor agreements (Business Associate Agreements and Data Processing Addendums) and may opt out of any de-identified-data use beyond Service delivery by providing written notice to privacy@soma-health.ca.

Section 2 — Breaches and Security Incidents

2.1 Reporting

2.1.1 Impermissible Use or Disclosure. SOMA will report to Covered Entity any use or disclosure of PHI not permitted by this BAA without unreasonable delay, and in no event later than thirty (30) days following discovery.

2.1.2 Breach of Unsecured PHI. SOMA will report to Covered Entity any Breach of Unsecured PHI without unreasonable delay, and in no case later than sixty (60) calendar days following discovery, consistent with 45 CFR § 164.410. Where the same incident also constitutes a Regulated Data Breach under SOMA's Data Processing Agreement, the seventy-two (72) hour notification commitment in that agreement applies in parallel and Covered Entity is entitled to the earlier notification. The report will include, to the extent then known:

  • A description of the nature of the Breach, including the dates of occurrence and discovery
  • The nature and extent of the PHI involved, including the categories of information and the likelihood of re-identification
  • The identity of the parties making or receiving the impermissible disclosure
  • Whether the PHI is reasonably believed to have been acquired or viewed
  • The corrective and investigative actions SOMA has taken or proposes to take
  • The risk assessment performed by SOMA in accordance with 45 CFR § 164.402
  • Any other information reasonably requested by Covered Entity

2.1.3 Security Incidents. SOMA will provide Covered Entity with a summary report of Security Incidents not resulting in an unauthorized disclosure of PHI on a quarterly basis. Security Incidents that result in unauthorized disclosure of PHI, or that constitute a Breach, will be reported under Sections 2.1.1 or 2.1.2 as applicable.

2.2 Mitigation

SOMA will take reasonable measures to mitigate, to the extent practicable, any harmful effect known to it of a use or disclosure of PHI by SOMA or its subcontractors in violation of this BAA.

2.3 Assistance with Notifications to Third Parties

SOMA will provide Covered Entity with reasonable assistance in preparing breach notifications required to be sent to affected individuals, regulators, or other third parties.

Section 3 — Access, Amendment, and Accounting

3.1 Access

SOMA will provide access to PHI in a Designated Record Set held by SOMA to Covered Entity (or, at Covered Entity's direction, to an individual) within a reasonable time and in a form that allows Covered Entity to meet its obligations under 45 CFR § 164.524.

3.2 Amendment

SOMA will make any amendment to PHI in a Designated Record Set that Covered Entity directs or agrees to pursuant to 45 CFR § 164.526, within a reasonable time after the request.

3.3 Accounting of Disclosures

SOMA will document and maintain a written record of disclosures of PHI made by SOMA that are not permitted under this BAA or the HIPAA Rules, including the date of disclosure, the name and address of the recipient, a brief description of the PHI disclosed, and the purpose of the disclosure. For repetitive disclosures to the same recipient for the same purpose, SOMA may record the details of the first disclosure together with the frequency, period, and number of disclosures and the date of the most recent disclosure.

3.4 Restriction Agreements and Confidential Communications

SOMA will comply with any restriction on the use or disclosure of PHI to which Covered Entity has agreed under 45 CFR § 164.522(a) and any confidential-communication requirement under 45 CFR § 164.522(b), provided Covered Entity has notified SOMA of the restriction in writing. Covered Entity will notify SOMA in writing of any termination of such a restriction.

3.5 Department of Health and Human Services

SOMA will make its internal practices, books, and records relating to the use and disclosure of PHI available to the Secretary of the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services for purposes of determining Covered Entity's compliance with the HIPAA Rules. This obligation continues during the term and for a period of five (5) years following termination of this BAA. SOMA will cooperate with regulatory investigations and will permit reasonable on-site access during business hours.

Section 4 — Term and Termination

4.1 Term

This BAA takes effect on the Effective Date and remains in effect for the duration of the Master Agreement, unless terminated earlier under Section 4.2. This BAA replaces any prior business associate agreement between the parties with respect to the Services.

4.2 Termination for Material Breach

If Covered Entity determines that SOMA has materially breached this BAA, Covered Entity will provide SOMA with written notice of the breach. SOMA will have thirty (30) days to cure. If the breach is not cured within that period, Covered Entity may terminate the Master Agreement and this BAA, immediately or as of a date specified in the notice.

4.3 Obligations upon Termination — Return or Destruction

Upon termination of this BAA, SOMA will return to Covered Entity, or destroy and permanently delete, all PHI received from, created on behalf of, or received by SOMA on behalf of Covered Entity, as promptly as reasonably possible. Where return or destruction is not feasible, SOMA will identify the categories of PHI for which return or destruction is infeasible and will limit further use of that PHI to the purposes that make return or destruction infeasible, continuing to apply the protections of this BAA for as long as the PHI is retained.

SOMA will provide written certification of the completion of the return or destruction, and will identify any PHI for which return or destruction was infeasible. SOMA will impose corresponding return, destruction, and certification obligations on its subcontractors that maintain PHI on its behalf. This Section 4.3 survives termination of this BAA.

4.4 Continuing Obligations

The privacy and security obligations of SOMA under this BAA survive termination with respect to any PHI retained under Section 4.3.

Section 5 — General Provisions

5.1 Relationship to Master Agreement

This BAA is incorporated into and forms part of the Master Agreement. In the event of a conflict between this BAA and the Master Agreement with respect to the handling of PHI, this BAA controls, unless the Master Agreement contains a provision more protective of PHI, in which case the more protective provision controls.

5.2 Notices

Notices required or permitted under this BAA will be in writing and delivered to the contacts identified by each party in the Master Agreement, or to such other address as a party designates in writing.

5.3 Change in Regulations; Amendment

The parties will use good-faith, commercially reasonable efforts to amend this BAA as needed to comply with amendments to the HIPAA Rules or other applicable law. References to regulatory citations include those citations as they may be amended from time to time.

5.4 Interpretation

Any ambiguity in this BAA will be resolved in favour of an interpretation that supports Covered Entity's compliance with the HIPAA Rules.

5.5 Binding Effect

This BAA binds and benefits the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

5.6 Counterparts

This BAA may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and together constitute one instrument. Electronic and digital signatures have the same effect as original signatures.

5.7 Severability

If any provision of this BAA is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

5.8 Governing Law

This BAA is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-law principles. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware for any dispute arising out of or relating to this BAA.

5.9 Waivers

No right or obligation under this BAA is waived except by an express written waiver signed by the party against whom the waiver is asserted.

5.10 Entire Agreement

This BAA, together with the Master Agreement, constitutes the entire agreement of the parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous understandings. It may be modified only by a writing signed by both parties.

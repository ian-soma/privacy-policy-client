Soma Mobile App Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 29, 2025

What this app does with your recordings

The Soma mobile app is designed for one thing: helping healthcare professionals record and transcribe therapy sessions in real-time. Here's exactly how we handle that.

Recording & Transcription

Before you record anything:

  • You must get explicit consent from your client
  • This is your responsibility as the professional
  • The app won't let you start without confirming consent

What happens to recordings:

  • Audio is NEVER stored—we process it in real-time only
  • As you speak, audio is sent securely to our servers
  • We use AI to convert speech to text instantly
  • The audio is immediately discarded after creating each text segment
  • Only the transcript text is saved to your account
  • We encrypt everything during transmission

The transcripts:

  • You get a text version of the conversation
  • Transcripts stay in your account until you delete them
  • You can review, edit, and use them for clinical notes
  • Important: Always review transcripts before using them officially—AI makes mistakes

What we collect through the app

When you record a session:

  • Audio data (processed in real-time, never stored)
  • Recording metadata (date, time, duration)
  • Client identifier (the alias you assigned, not their real name)
  • Which client the recording belongs to

Technical information:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App performance data (crashes, errors)
  • Your login information

We keep clients anonymous

This is important: We don't know who your clients are. The app uses aliases that you create. Please don't use real names or identifying information in the alias—that's on you to keep anonymous.

How we protect recordings

  • End-to-end encryption during transmission
  • Real-time processing only—no audio storage means no audio storage risk
  • Secure transcription—text is encrypted and stored securely
  • Limited access—only authorized AI systems process the audio
  • No human listening—your recordings aren't reviewed by staff unless there's a technical issue you report

What we DON'T do

  • ❌ We don't store audio files
  • ❌ We don't sell recordings or transcripts
  • ❌ We don't share them with advertisers
  • ❌ We don't use them to train AI models (without explicit consent)
  • ❌ We don't share them with anyone except you

If something goes wrong

If there's a data breach affecting your transcripts:

  • We'll tell you immediately
  • We'll explain what happened
  • We'll help you figure out what notifications you need to make
  • We take this very seriously

Note: Since we don't store audio, there's no audio to breach—only transcripts.

Your professional responsibilities

Remember:

  • You need to get consent before recording
  • You need to follow your professional ethics and local laws
  • You should review transcripts before putting them in official records
  • You are responsible for how you use the transcripts

This app is a tool—you're still the professional in charge.

Data retention

  • Audio: Never stored—processed in real-time and immediately discarded
  • Transcripts: Kept until you delete your account or remove them
  • After you delete your account: All data is permanently deleted

Your rights

You can:

  • Stop using the app and delete your account anytime
  • Request to see what transcripts we have
  • Delete individual transcripts
  • Export your transcripts

Technical details

  • Servers: Located in North America
  • Compliance: We follow Canadian privacy laws (PIPEDA)
  • Healthcare standards: We follow healthcare data protection practices
  • Not a medical device: This app assists with documentation—it doesn't diagnose or treat

Updates to this policy

We'll update this if our practices change. If we make changes that reduce your privacy protections, we'll email you 30 days in advance.

Soma Professional App — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 25, 2026  |  Version: 2.0

1. What This App Does

The Soma Professional mobile app is a session recording and documentation tool built for therapists, counselors, and other licensed practitioners. It allows you to:

  • Record sessions in real time (with explicit client consent)
  • Generate transcripts automatically as you speak
  • Access those transcripts through the Soma web platform to draft clinical notes
  • Protect client identity through an alias system that avoids using real names

This policy covers only the Soma Professional mobile app (iOS and Android). For the full Soma platform, including the web application and client-facing mobile app, please see our complete Privacy Policy at soma-health.ca/privacy.

3. What Data We Collect

The following categories are organized for clarity and to satisfy app-store privacy nutrition label requirements.

3.1 Recording Data

  • Audio stream — captured from the device microphone during an active recording session. Audio is processed in real time and is never stored (see Section 4).
  • Transcript text — the text output generated from the real-time audio stream, saved to your Soma account.

3.2 Session Metadata

  • Date and time of the recording
  • Duration of the session
  • Client alias (the identifier you assign — not the client's real name)

3.3 Account Data

  • Name and email address
  • Professional designation (if provided)
  • Authentication credentials (securely hashed)

3.4 Technical Data

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App version and performance data (crash reports, error logs)
  • General connection quality metrics (to ensure transcription reliability)

3.5 Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Location data (GPS, Wi-Fi-based, or Bluetooth-based)
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, or other on-device content
  • Cross-app tracking identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Apple HealthKit or Google Health Connect data (if wearable integration is introduced in the future, this policy will be updated with explicit consent requirements before any data is accessed)

4. How Audio Is Processed

Audio is NEVER stored. This is the cornerstone of our privacy design.

Here is exactly what happens when you press Record:

  1. The app captures audio from your device microphone.
  2. Small audio segments are transmitted in real time over an encrypted connection (TLS 1.2+) to our processing servers hosted in Canadian data centers.
  3. Our AI transcription service converts each audio segment to text.
  4. The audio segment is immediately discarded after the text is generated. No audio file, buffer, or recording is retained on our servers, on your device, or anywhere else.
  5. Only the resulting transcript text is saved to your account.

Because audio is never stored, there is no audio file that could be breached, subpoenaed, or accessed by anyone — including us.

5. Client Anonymity

The Soma Professional app uses an alias system to protect client identity. When you create a client profile, you assign an alias — a label of your choosing — rather than the client's legal name.

  • We do not ask for, require, or store your client's real name, date of birth, or any other personal identifier.
  • The alias is the only identifier associated with session transcripts in our system.
  • We strongly recommend that you do not use real names, initials, or any identifying information as aliases.

This means that even in the unlikely event of a data breach, transcript data cannot easily be linked to an identifiable individual.

6. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect for the following purposes:

  • Provide the service — process audio into transcripts and deliver them to your account
  • Enable clinical documentation — make transcripts available on the Soma web platform for note drafting
  • Maintain and improve reliability — monitor app performance, diagnose crashes, and fix errors
  • Product improvement — with your explicit, separate consent only, we may use anonymized, aggregated data to improve transcription accuracy
  • Communicate with you — send service-related notices (downtime, policy changes, security alerts)

We do not use your data to build user profiles for advertising, and we do not use session content for any purpose beyond what is listed above.

7. AI Processing & Transparency

Soma uses artificial intelligence to convert audio to text and to assist with clinical note generation. We believe you deserve to know exactly how this works.

  • Transcription provider: Audio-to-text processing is handled by our AI infrastructure. Audio segments are processed in real time and discarded immediately.
  • Note generation: When you use the web platform to generate draft clinical notes from a transcript, the text is processed by enterprise-grade AI models hosted within our managed cloud infrastructure.
  • Zero-day AI retention: Data sent to our AI infrastructure is processed in real time and not stored, cached, or retained by the AI provider after the response is delivered. Your session data is never used to train, fine-tune, or improve any AI models unless you have explicitly opted in. This opt-in setting is off by default. These are contractual requirements enforced through our data processing agreements.
  • On-device vs. cloud: Audio capture occurs on your device. All transcription processing occurs in the cloud via encrypted connections. No AI processing runs on-device.

8. What We Do NOT Do

  • ✗ We do not store audio recordings — ever.
  • ✗ We do not sell transcripts, recordings, or any personal data.
  • ✗ We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • ✗ We do not use your data to train AI models without your explicit opt-in consent (off by default).
  • ✗ We do not track you across other apps or websites (no cross-app tracking).
  • ✗ We do not collect advertising identifiers or participate in ad networks.
  • ✗ We do not have staff listen to your recordings (audio is never stored to listen to).
  • ✗ We do not share session content with anyone other than you, the account holder.

9. Data Sharing

Your transcript and session data is shared only in the following, limited circumstances:

  • With your own account on the Soma web platform — transcripts generated in the app sync to your account on the web platform so you can draft clinical notes. This is the core function of the service.
  • With our AI processing partners — as described in Section 7, audio segments and transcript text are processed by our AI infrastructure under strict data processing agreements that prohibit retention or model training.
  • If required by law — we may disclose data if compelled by a valid court order, subpoena, or legal process under Canadian law. We will notify you unless legally prohibited from doing so.

We do not share data with any other third parties. We do not operate analytics SDKs that transmit data to third-party services.

10. Security

We take the security of your data seriously and employ the following measures:

  • Encryption in transit: All data transmitted between the app and our servers uses TLS 1.2 or higher. Audio segments are encrypted end-to-end during real-time transmission.
  • Encryption at rest: Transcripts and account data stored on our servers are encrypted using AES-256 encryption.
  • Canadian data residency: All data is processed and stored in Canadian data centers.
  • Access controls: Server access is restricted to authorized personnel through multi-factor authentication and role-based permissions.
  • No audio storage: Because audio is never stored, there is no audio data at rest to protect — eliminating an entire category of risk.
  • Secure authentication: Passwords are salted and hashed. We support secure session management and token-based authentication.

11. Data Retention

Data Type Retention Period
Audio Never stored. Processed in real time and immediately discarded.
Transcripts Retained until you delete them or close your account.
Session metadata (date, time, duration, alias) Retained alongside the transcript for as long as the transcript exists.
Technical logs (device type, OS, app performance) 90 days, then automatically deleted.
Account data Duration of your active account plus 60 days after account closure, then permanently deleted.

When you delete a transcript, it is permanently removed from our systems. When you close your account, all associated data (transcripts, metadata, and account information) is permanently deleted within 60 days.

12. Your Rights

Under PIPEDA and applicable provincial privacy legislation, you have the right to:

  • Access your data — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Delete your data — delete individual transcripts from within the app, or request full account deletion. An in-app deletion mechanism is available in your account settings.
  • Export your data — download your transcripts in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent — stop using the recording feature or close your account at any time.
  • Correct inaccuracies — request correction of any inaccurate personal information.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@soma-health.ca. We will respond to your request within 30 calendar days. If we need additional time, we will notify you of the reason and the expected timeline.

13. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach that creates a real risk of significant harm:

  • We will notify the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (OPC) within 72 hours of confirming the breach.
  • We will notify affected users as soon as feasible, including a description of the breach, the types of data involved, and the steps we are taking.
  • We will keep records of all breaches, regardless of severity, for a minimum of 24 months as required by PIPEDA.
  • We will provide guidance on any steps you may need to take regarding your professional obligations (such as notifying your regulatory college or clients).

Because audio is never stored, a breach cannot expose audio recordings — only transcript text and account data could potentially be affected.

14. Your Professional Responsibilities

Soma is a documentation tool that supports your clinical workflow. As the licensed practitioner, you retain full professional responsibility for:

  • Obtaining consent: You must obtain explicit, informed consent from your client before initiating any recording. The app requires you to confirm that consent has been obtained before a session can begin.
  • Reviewing AI-generated content: Transcripts and any AI-assisted clinical notes are drafts. You are responsible for reviewing, editing, and approving all content before it becomes part of an official clinical record.
  • Regulatory compliance: You are responsible for ensuring your use of this tool complies with your professional regulatory body's standards, your jurisdiction's privacy laws, and any applicable record-keeping requirements.
  • Client alias management: You are responsible for ensuring that aliases do not contain identifying information about your clients.

This app does not diagnose, treat, or provide clinical recommendations. It is a session recording and documentation tool.

15. Children's Privacy

The Soma Professional app is designed for use by licensed practitioners and clinical professionals. It is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

If a practitioner uses the app to record a session involving a minor client, the practitioner is solely responsible for obtaining appropriate parental or guardian consent as required by their jurisdiction and professional standards.

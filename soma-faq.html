SOMA HEALTH SOLUTIONS INC. — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Updated: August 25, 2026

These are the questions clinicians actually ask us, answered plainly. Soma is built for psychologists, therapists and counsellors, and this page is written for you — not for your IT department. For the formal versions of anything here, see our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

About Soma

What is Soma?

A drafting workspace for clinical work. You bring in documents, record or transcribe sessions, chat about a case, and draft assessment reports, notes, summaries and letters — then the finished work goes into your own records system. Soma is deliberately not a records system: everything in it has a destroy date, and nothing in it is intended to be the copy you keep.

Is Soma a medical device?

No. Soma is not classified as a medical device in any jurisdiction, including by Health Canada or the FDA. It does not make clinical decisions — every output is a draft for a professional who retains full responsibility for client care.

What stage is Soma at?

Soma is live. Canadian clinicians use it in real practice today — drafting assessment reports, notes, summaries and letters — and the product improves continuously, with clinician feedback driving the roadmap.

Reports

Can AI write clinical and psychological reports?

Yes — as drafts for you. You bring in your assessment data and source documents, and Soma produces a complete, organized draft that you review, edit and sign. The draft is written in your voice and clinical style, not generic AI language, and each section is checked against your source documents. You remain the clinician of record.

How does the report generator work?

Four steps: upload a reference report you are happy with (Soma builds your template from it — your exact sections, reading level, length and voice), upload the case files, review the draft section by section, then sign and export. The AI drafting step is de-identified and zero-retention — your clients’ data is never stored by the model or used to train it.

Will the report sound like me?

Yes. Give Soma one of your past reports and it matches your sentence structure, vocabulary and phrasing, so the draft needs far less editing to sound like you. Because you review, edit and sign every page, the final report reflects your clinical judgment — not a template’s.

What goes into a report, and does it check scores?

You provide the source documents and clinical input; Soma organizes them into a review-ready draft with consistent sections, and checks every score it writes against your documents. It is built to remove the manual assembly of a report, not to replace your clinical judgment.

What if a report is very long?

Reports run up to your plan’s page cap, and a long report still counts as one report. Uploaded files can be up to 500 pages. Revisions, reprompts and edits never count as a new report.

Can I export reports and notes to Word (DOCX)?

Yes. Reports and notes export as DOCX, so they drop straight into your existing documentation workflow and your own records system.

Notes and chat

What note formats does Soma support?

A library of more than 20 formats: the four standard structures — SOAP, DAP, BIRP and GIRP — plus freeform progress notes and modality- and population-specific templates (EMDR, DBT, IFS, couples, play therapy, addiction counselling and more). Custom templates can be built for your own documentation needs, so the note matches how you actually write.

How does Soma speed up documentation?

Soma produces the first draft so you spend your time reviewing instead of writing from a blank page. Notes are drafted in minutes from your session transcription, in the format you choose, and processing data is destroyed after export.

What is Soma’s chat?

Your own private AI agent — it has read the documents in your case and carries out work for you. Summarize a report, ask what an earlier assessment concluded, draft a letter. It chats about your documents, drafts what you ask, and runs your workflow. The conversation is ephemeral — nothing from it is stored, and it never trains on your clients’ data.

Your clients’ information

Who owns the information I bring into Soma?

You do. Documents, notes, chats and drafts remain your information and your clients’ information. Soma is a tool you use to work on it, not a party that acquires it. We never sell it, never share it with third parties for their own purposes, and when you delete something it is genuinely removed, not archived for our benefit.

Do you sell data to anyone?

No. Soma does not sell, trade, or monetize personal, health, or usage data. Third parties are involved only to deliver the service itself, under strict confidentiality and data-protection agreements — zero retention, and no training on your data.

Where is my clients’ information stored?

In Canada. Documents, notes, chats and drafts live encrypted in Canadian data centres, and the de-identification step that protects them runs in Canada too. The most sensitive content carries an additional layer of encryption with keys we hold, not our infrastructure providers.

Does the AI ever see identifying details?

No. Identifying details — names, dates of birth, contact details, health card and file numbers, addresses — are removed before any AI model sees your text, and replaced with placeholders. Documents are de-identified when they arrive, and you review and confirm the redactions yourself before content goes anywhere: automatic detection is good, not perfect, and your confirmation is what turns a claim into a process. Do your part as well: refer to clients by alias, never by name, in chat and anywhere you type in Soma.

Does Soma store my clients’ names?

No. Soma never asks for a client’s name and the product has nowhere to put one. Cases are titled by you — a file number or a label. Recordings are grouped under an alias. Documents are de-identified for your review before anything is drafted from them. What you type is up to you, which is why keeping names out is part of the workflow rather than a promise made on your behalf.

Who can see my cases?

You. Your workspace is yours alone — other clinicians cannot see your cases, and there is no sharing feature that could expose them by accident. On our side, nobody browses customer content: sensitive content is encrypted, our staff work from system logs and error reports that are filtered so they cannot quote your text, and any access for support happens with your knowledge.

How long is my work kept?

By default, 30 days after you last touch something — and you can shorten or lengthen that in your settings. Soma is a temporary workspace by design, so forgetting is a feature. A daily sweep removes chats, cases and drafts that have passed your retention window — the stored content itself, not just the listing. Each case shows the date it would clear, so nothing disappears without having said so first.

What happens when I delete something?

It is removed from our systems immediately — the content, not just the entry in a list. There is no recycle bin and no “soft delete” that quietly keeps things. Deletion is permanent and there is no recovery: that is the point of it. Export finished work into your own records while the case is live.

What is my role under privacy law?

Under PIPEDA and provincial health privacy law, you remain the custodian of your clients’ information — Soma is a service provider processing it on your instructions. Our end: Canadian storage and processing, encryption, de-identification before AI, access controls, retention that actually deletes, breach monitoring, and no secondary use of your data. Your end: custody of the finished record, and any client consent your practice and its regulatory context call for.

What if something goes wrong?

We plan for it rather than pretend it cannot happen. The design rule is that failure defaults to safe: if de-identification is unavailable, messages are refused rather than sent raw, and if a document cannot be verified as redacted, it does not move forward. Monitoring runs on our own infrastructure, with error reports filtered so they cannot quote your content. And your review step exists precisely because no automated safeguard is perfect. If an incident ever affects your data, you hear it from us — promptly, plainly, and with what it means for you, in line with PIPEDA’s breach requirements.

AI and training

Is my data used to train AI?

No outside AI provider ever trains on your data — that is contractual, and it is Section 11.4 of our Terms of Service. Your clients’ data is never used to train any model, ours or anyone else’s — identifiable or anonymized. Soma may use anonymized, aggregated data to improve its own, non-third-party models — never client content — as permitted by Section 4.5 of the same Terms.

What was the AI trained on?

Soma builds on established enterprise large-language models trained by their providers on publicly available and licensed datasets. Soma’s own components are trained exclusively on anonymized, aggregated data — never on client content. Soma has not released any model trained on user data.

Can I train the AI on my own preferences?

Not directly, yet. Your templates and editing patterns shape how Soma adapts to your workflow, and personalized preferences — note structure, terminology — and memory are in active development.

Are some populations underrepresented in AI systems?

Like most large AI systems, underlying training data may underrepresent certain cultures, languages, or marginalized groups. Soma is actively building validation methods — including practitioner surveys — to identify and address bias or blind spots. Your clinical review of every draft is, deliberately, part of the safeguard — and please use the feedback system inside Soma to make us aware of the changes you would like to see.

Does Soma diagnose, predict, or recommend treatment?

No. Soma does not suggest or infer diagnoses, does not predict outcomes, and does not generate treatment recommendations. Its job is drafting and documentation. Anything that reads as clinical opinion is a draft for your review — clinical judgment stays where it belongs, with you.

Does the AI act on its own?

No. Soma’s AI has no independent agency. It generates content only in response to your instruction, cannot initiate actions, and cannot make decisions. Every output is subject to your review and approval before use.

How accurate are the drafts?

We believe Soma produces the best first draft you can get from software — well-structured, grounded in your own documents and templates, and close enough to done that most drafts need little editing. Early results bear that out, and every release raises the bar. It remains a drafting aid, not a substitute for professional judgment: all AI can make mistakes, so review everything before it enters a clinical or client-facing context.

Working in Soma

Can I close my laptop in the middle of a report?

Yes. Report generation runs on our servers, not in your browser — close the tab, come back tomorrow. Sections are written and saved one at a time, so progress is never lost, and a stalled report can be resumed without losing anything already written. Recording a session is the one exception: that runs in the browser, so leaving the page ends the recording.

Is Soma my clinical record system?

No — and it should not be. Soma is where the work gets drafted; your records system is where the finished work lives. Everything in Soma clears itself after your retention window, and nothing in it satisfies a record-keeping obligation on its own. When a draft is done, export it and file it where your records live.

Safety and compliance

What safeguards are in place?

  • De-identification before AI, with your review and confirmation of redactions;
  • Fail-safe design — if a safeguard is unavailable, work stops rather than proceeding unprotected;
  • Encryption in transit and at rest, with an additional key layer on the most sensitive content;
  • Retention that actually deletes, on a schedule you control;
  • Audit logging of system activity for transparency and accountability;
  • Monitoring on our own infrastructure, with error reports filtered so they cannot quote your content.

Is Soma designed to comply with regulatory standards?

Yes. Soma is built around Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA and provincial frameworks such as PHIPA), with third-party HIPAA compliance review under way for US practice. The platform is built to evolve with regulatory change, with legal oversight and regular internal review.
